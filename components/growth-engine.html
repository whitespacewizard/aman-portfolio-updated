Growth workflow

A connected system from research to revenue

The Quick Clean demand-generation workflow, mapped as a live operating system: account research, qualification, enrichment, signal-led outreach, governed multichannel follow-up, reply triage, nurture and sales handoff.

◈ Agent makes or supports a decision ⛨ Governor can pause outreach upstream ⋯ Tool is available to a connected agent ↩ Loop recycles nurture and feeds outcomes back
  1. Research accounts using Claude, Gemini and directories, then verify and qualify the ICP.
  2. Identify stakeholders, enrich contacts through Apollo and fallback sources, then store valid records in Zoho CRM.
  3. Route event, website and LinkedIn sources into the same CRM holding pool.
  4. Watch for signals, draft relevant outreach, require human approval where needed, and run a governed multichannel cadence.
  5. Triage replies into meetings, human review, suppression or nurture, then feed outcomes back into qualification.
