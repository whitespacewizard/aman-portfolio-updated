FAQ

Questions before we work together

A few quick answers about how I approach growth, collaboration and project execution.

What kind of growth work do you specialise in?

I work across GTM strategy, demand generation, outbound systems, performance marketing, lifecycle campaigns, conversion funnels and marketing automation for B2B and D2C businesses.

Can you build a growth function from scratch?

Yes. I can help define the ICP, sharpen positioning, build campaign infrastructure, create acquisition workflows, align marketing with sales and establish reporting around pipeline and revenue.

Do you work with both B2B and D2C businesses?

Yes. My experience covers B2B pipeline generation, outbound and GTM, along with D2C acquisition, paid media, Shopify CRO and customer journey optimisation.

How do you measure whether a campaign is working?

I connect campaign activity to business outcomes such as qualified leads, pipeline, conversion rates, acquisition cost, ROAS and revenue. The exact metrics depend on the business model and funnel.

Can you collaborate with an existing marketing or sales team?

Yes. I regularly work with founders, leadership, sales, creative, content and performance teams to create clear priorities, shared workflows and measurable execution plans.

What is the best way to start a conversation?

Share a short note about your business, current growth challenge and expected outcome. You can email me at amanraj.gtm@gmail.com.
