06 — Email Sequence

1 initial + 3 follow-ups. Plain text. No links.

Email 1
Initial
Longest. Full formula. 10 AM (US) / 8 AM (UK).
Email 2
Offer Reminder
Short. Complementary design. Curiosity. 12 PM.
Email 3
Urgency
"October is closer than it looks." Time pressure.
Email 4
Breakup
"Last note from me." Triggers final response.
Critical Rule
Zero links in any email
Links trigger spam. Every email is plain text. Opt-out is a human sentence: "If you already have your builder, let me know and I won't follow up."
Why 4 not 5?
"After the third email they just leave — they don't read. So we reduced from five to four."
07 — Copy Formula

Five elements, every email, every time.

1
Why am I reaching out?
"I was looking at the floor plan for one of our customers and noticed [Company]." Context — not random.
2
Why is this important for you?
"With 3,400 exhibitors, the stands that start the right conversations are the ones built to stand out."
3
Who are we?
"We're Reaction Space — we've built for [Client Names]." Keep it to 1–2 lines. Credibility, not a pitch deck.
4
What are we offering?
"I'd like to offer a complementary design concept." Free design — paid service given away as the hook.
5
Call to Action
"Can we connect tomorrow?" — Never "this week." Specific. Urgent. Direct.

09 — Anti-ChatGPT Policy

AI copy is killing deliverability.

The Problem
"I noticed, I noticed, I noticed" — Google has flagged this pattern. Thousands of agencies using same GPT prompts = identical output = spam folder. Instantly's own system detects AI-generated copy.
The Policy
GPT for research only. Understand the event, the industry. Then rewrite everything in your own words. Tarik spent 2 months training a custom GPT — still only uses it for scaffolding. Final copy must sound like a human wrote it.

"We are not the only agency — there are thousands. They all use ChatGPT. So ChatGPT is just a template. Google has already flagged that pattern. You never hit the inbox."
